# Marketing and positioning (marketing)

## Positioning

InteractiveAgents is a lightweight alternative to larger agent platforms:

- focused on Unity integration;
- quick to start without additional infrastructure;
- fully configurable roles and personas.

## Audiences

- Unity teams at agencies and studios;
- B2B product teams with demonstration requirements;
- event and trade-show agencies.

## Example messages

- "Bring your experts into the showroom as NPCs."
- "Start in minutes and extend later without unnecessary overhead."
- "Use a local knowledge base and retain control over its content."

## Campaign ideas

- demonstration booth: "Talking product experts"
- webinar: "Build an interactive booth in 30 minutes"
