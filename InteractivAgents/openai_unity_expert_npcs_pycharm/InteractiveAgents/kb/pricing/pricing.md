# Prices and plans (pricing)

## Plans

- **Starter**: one room, up to three agents, and a local knowledge base of up
  to 50 files. Example price: EUR 199 per month, cancellable monthly.
- **Pro**: five rooms, up to 15 agents, and a local knowledge base of up to 500
  files. Example price: EUR 699 per month, billed annually.
- **Enterprise**: unlimited rooms, service-level agreement, and priority
  support. Example price: from EUR 2,400 per month.

## Add-ons

- two-hour onboarding workshop: EUR 450 once
- custom voice or animation hook: EUR 300 once
- dedicated support channel: EUR 250 per month

## Example discounts

- startup less than two years old: 15% on Starter or Pro
- three-month event bundle: 10%
- nonprofit organization: 20% after review

## Example delivery times

- standard setup: two to three business days
- custom integration: one to three weeks, depending on scope
